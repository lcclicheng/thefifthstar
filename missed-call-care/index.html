Straight answers

Things owners ask first.

How does the missed-call text-back work?

We connect to your business number (or a forwarding number we set up). When a call goes unanswered, an SMS goes out in your voice with a book-or-reply link. You stay the owner of the number.

Do you answer my chat in real time?

On Growth and Pro, yes — we manage the chat widget in your tone during cover hours, with FAQ auto-replies handling the common asks. Pro runs 24/7 so the late enquiry is caught.

Is this the same as GBP Care?

No — GBP Care looks after your Google reviews. Missed-Call & Chat Care looks after the live enquiry: the call that rang and the chat that opened. They reinforce each other — more reviews come from more happy customers you actually caught.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Month-to-month, no contract, no exit fee. Email me and it stops at the end of the current month.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. The text-back and chat replies are written in your business's voice by the same person who writes your review replies — so a customer can't tell the difference between you and us, which is the point.

Is £79+ worth it versus doing it myself?

One recovered booking a month pays for it. Most owners lose far more than that to silent calls and dead chats. One person, no overhead, same voice as your replies and your site.
