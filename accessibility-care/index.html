The invisible wall

Your site quietly shuts people out.

You don’t lose customers in one dramatic moment. You lose them the moment a screen reader can’t book, or a form can’t be filled.

1 · The customer who can’t get in

A keyboard-only user, a screen-reader user, low vision. Your menu image has no label, your button has no name. They hit a wall, bounce, and book the competitor whose site lets them through.

2 · The fine risk

Under the EU Act, non-compliance can mean penalties up to €5m or 5% of turnover. Most owners have no idea their site fails the standard — until a complaint or a check lands.

Here’s the thing: accessibility isn’t a favour, it’s the law now — and it’s also just good business. An open site earns the customers a closed one loses.
