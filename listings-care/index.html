Straight answers

Things owners ask first.

Do you log into my directory accounts?

You add FifthStar as a manager or grant access on each platform (you stay the owner). That's the safe way — you can remove us in two clicks, anytime.

Which directories do you cover?

Starter covers 4 core ones (Bing, Apple Maps, Yell, Facebook). Growth adds Yelp & TripAdvisor. Pro covers all major UK directories plus trade/category sites relevant to your business.

Is this the same as GBP Care?

No — GBP Care looks after your Google Business Profile (reviews, posts, photos). Listings Care keeps your name, address and phone correct across every other directory. Most businesses run both; they reinforce each other.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Month-to-month, no contract, no exit fee. Email me and it stops at the end of the current month.

Do you guarantee I'll rank higher?

No honest provider does. Consistent listings are a proven local-search trust signal; we put the consistency in place so the trend goes the right way. You stay in control the whole time.

Is £59+ worth it versus doing it myself?

Keeping 8+ directories in sync takes real hours, and one wrong field quietly costs customers. One person, no overhead, same voice as your replies and your site. You're not paying for an account manager you'll never meet.
