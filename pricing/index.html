How the welcome rate works

Your first 12 months run at the lower rate shown on each plan (e.g. GBP Care Growth is £239/mo instead of £299/mo). After month 12 the standard rate applies. You can cancel before then, anytime, no penalty.

Founding-customer legacy price

The first Bristol businesses we worked with are locked at £39/mo for GBP Care Reply-only, permanently — a thank-you for being early. New Bristol enquiries are quoted at the standard welcome rate.

You don't pay until you've seen it

Every care plan starts with a free check, audit or sample (your GBP check, website review, listings check, accessibility audit, or missed-enquiry sample). You only start a paid plan once you've seen the plan or report and said yes.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your business and we'll point you to the right starting point — usually one plan, sometimes two. No pressure, no call booked by default.
