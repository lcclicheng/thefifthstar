Straight answers

Things owners ask first.

Do you post replies for me?

No. Replies go from your own Google account — that's both the Google rule and the safe way. We write them; you approve with one paste.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Three replies, no card, no account. If it's useful, we talk about the next step — only when you're ready.

What if I only want one service?

Each Care Plan stands on its own. Pick GBP Care, Website Care, Listings Care, Accessibility Care or Missed-Call & Chat Care — or stack them. No bundle required.

Who actually writes the replies?

An AI draft in your tone, written by someone who's read your reviews. You stay in control and can tweak any line before posting.

What about my data?

We only use review text that's already public on Google. You stay in control of your account the whole time. GDPR-aware by design.
